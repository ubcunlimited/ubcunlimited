# UBC Unlimited — Design Brainstorm

## Context
Utah-based merchant services provider. Target audience: small-to-mid-size business owners (restaurants, retail, medical, automotive, salons). Trust, clarity, and local credibility are paramount. The brand uses navy + teal + gold.

<text>
**Idea A — Corporate Precision (Probability: 0.07)**

- **Design Movement:** Swiss International Style meets fintech clarity
- **Core Principles:** Grid-locked structure, data-forward hierarchy, institutional trust signals, zero decorative noise
- **Color Philosophy:** Deep navy (#0a1628) as authority, teal (#169fa8) as action, white space as premium signal
- **Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric two-column grids with a dominant left-rail text column and a right-side visual/data panel. No centered hero blobs.
- **Signature Elements:** Thin horizontal rule dividers, monospaced stat callouts, tight-tracked uppercase labels
- **Interaction Philosophy:** Subtle underline reveals on hover, no scale transforms — precision over playfulness
- **Animation:** Staggered fade-up on scroll (50ms delay per item), no bounces
- **Typography:** Sora 800 for headings (tight tracking), DM Sans 400/500 for body
</text>

- **Core Principles:** Warm neutrals with navy anchors, editorial section breaks, human photography integration, trust-first hierarchy

- **Interaction Philosophy:** Slow, deliberate hover transitions (300ms), card lifts with warm shadows
- **Animation:** Horizontal slide-in from left for section titles, opacity fade for body text
- **Typography:** Playfair Display 700 for headings, Inter 400 for body

**Idea C — Modern Fintech Edge (Probability: 0.08)**

- **Design Movement:** SaaS fintech — Stripe/Linear aesthetic applied to local services
- **Core Principles:** Dark navy hero sections, glassy card components, teal gradient accents, high information density with breathing room
- **Color Philosophy:** Navy-950 (#040c1c) hero backgrounds create depth; teal gradients signal innovation; gold badges signal premium; white cards float on dark sections
- **Layout Paradigm:** Full-bleed dark hero → white content sections → alternating dark/light bands. Cards use subtle glassmorphism on dark backgrounds.
- **Signature Elements:** Gradient text on key headlines, glassy stat cards with backdrop blur, teal glow on primary CTAs
- **Interaction Philosophy:** Card hover lifts with teal border glow, button shimmer on hover, smooth section transitions
- **Animation:** Entrance animations with framer-motion (slide-up + fade, 0.4s ease-out), staggered grid items
- **Typography:** Sora 800 for hero headings, Inter 400/500 for body — strong weight contrast

## Selected Design: **Idea C — Modern Fintech Edge**

Dark navy hero sections with glassy card components, teal gradient accents, and high information density. This approach positions UBC Unlimited as a sophisticated, modern provider while maintaining the trust signals needed for a local Utah audience.
